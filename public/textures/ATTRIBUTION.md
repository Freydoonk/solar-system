# Surface maps

The bundled `2k_*.jpg` and `2k_saturn_ring_alpha.png` images are by **Solar System Scope / INOVE**.

- Source: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Downloaded 19 October 2026 from the source page's `textures/download/` links. Original filenames and image data are unchanged.
- The maps are based on NASA imagery/elevation data, with adjusted colors and reconstructed areas. They are static illustrations, not date-specific observations.

Bundled maps: Mercury, Venus atmosphere, Earth day/night/clouds, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn and rings, Uranus, Neptune, Sun, Moon.

The Pluto and other satellite maps are generated by this application's code as artistic approximations. They are not observed surface maps. The slender Uranus ring is illustrative. Body pole directions and spin rates come from Astronomy Engine; map prime-meridian alignment and cloud weather are illustrative.

Attribution is also displayed in the application's controls and README. Keep this attribution when distributing the maps.
