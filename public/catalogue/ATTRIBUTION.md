# Offline sky catalogue

The bundled star subset is derived from **HYG 4.1**, David Nash / Astronexus, combining Hipparcos, Yale Bright Star and Gliese data. Source: https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database/tree/main/hyg/CURRENT . Current upstream: https://codeberg.org/astronexus/hyg . HYG and this adapted subset are licensed **CC BY-SA 4.0**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . Changes: selected visual magnitude <= 6 (excluding the Sun); retained names, identifiers, J2000 coordinates, Cartesian position and velocity; converted CSV to JSON. Name and magnitude values are catalogue values, not live brightness measurements.

Constellation line patterns, names and the 110 Messier object records come from **D3-Celestial**, Olaf Frohn: https://github.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial/tree/master/data . The BSD 3-Clause copyright, conditions and disclaimer are included in [D3-LICENSE.txt](D3-LICENSE.txt). Changes: retained only line geometry/name fields and the Messier collection; converted to compact JSON. These conventional line patterns are not IAU constellation boundaries. Serpens is represented in two parts.

Data retrieved 26 October 2026. Regenerate with `node scripts/build-sky-catalogue.mjs` (network required for this maintenance command only). Data is bundled in the lazy atlas chunk and precached; no runtime catalogue requests are made. The chart applies HYG space motion and precession/nutation; it omits stellar annual aberration/parallax and atmospheric refraction. It is a wide-field finding aid, not precision astrometry or a prediction of visual detectability.
